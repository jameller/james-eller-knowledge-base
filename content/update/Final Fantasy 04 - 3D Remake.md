# Personal Thoughts
This remake is a personal highlight for me. I love [[Final Fantasy 04|Final Fantasy IV]] and that story coupled with this aesthetic style really brings the best of both worlds together. Some fans complain about the voice-acting, but I think the almost Shakespearean overacting only adds to the melodrama that is at the heart of this game. There is a significant new mechanic centering on formerly character exclusive abilities known as the Augment ability system. It is a direct impact on gameplay, but doesn't detract in any way for me. All in all, I think this is the superior way to play [[Final Fantasy 04|Final Fantasy IV]].

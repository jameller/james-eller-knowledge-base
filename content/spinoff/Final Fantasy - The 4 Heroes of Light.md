# Personal Thoughts
This is an odd little spinoff title. It feels like a modern interpretation of the style and storylines from early mainline entries such as Final Fantasy and Final Fantasy III. It features a unique art style and spawned a spiritual successor series called Bravely Default. According to game producer Tomoya Asano in a [2014 interview by Peter Brown on Gamespot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/bravely-default-from-sequel-to-sequel/1100-6417605/), “Bravely Default was originally envisioned as a sequel to a preexisting game from another series: Final Fantasy: The 4 Heroes of Light.”

While this game is often considered to be merely the seed for Bravely Default, I find that that undervalues what it does well. The story’s narrow scope and simple characterization allowed the team to really lean into aesthetics and it nails the details. It’s a small, rich, dense world that’s lovely to experience!

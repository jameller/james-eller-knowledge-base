# Significance
Final Fantasy VIII is generally looked down upon and considered the punching bag of the franchise. That’s not to say that it doesn’t have any fans, but they are a minority within the fan community. Perhaps in an attempt to ‘correct’ the character models from [[Final Fantasy 07|FF7]], the team attempted a hyper realistic design on hardware that simply couldn’t match the desired fidelity. As a result, the graphics in this entry have aged particularly poorly. Furthermore, the central progression system known as the junction system, is very easy to exploit. This leads to trivialized, and ultimately unnecessary, random combat encounters that slow gameplay down. To pile on further, the story is often viewed as convoluted and the time-travel shenanigans only compound this further. Ultimately, at its core, it’s a love story between two of the player characters but this leaves the world-threatening antagonist feeling secondary. There is potential here; the scaffolding of a genre re-defining experience. Unfortunately, the poor execution caused this game to be the first major miss in the golden age of the series. Square decided to return to their roots in the next entry and have mostly left Final Fantasy VII behind.

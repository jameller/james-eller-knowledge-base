# Significance
Final Fantasy VII is no longer simply an entry, it’s spawned a franchise of its own that’s further elaborated on in the [[seven-derivative/index|seven derivative section]]. It’s hard to overstate the impact this game has had on the video game industry. The polygonal graphics are unlike anything created before or after. They’re idiosyncratic and immediately recognizable. That isn’t to say that everyone agrees that they’re good graphics, but they are truly unique. Furthermore, the pre-rendered backgrounds blend eastern urbanism, dieselpunk, and glorified naturalism in such a way that I struggle to find anything comparable. It’s a strange gestalt, and one must experience the set pieces themselves to truly understand how it all fits together. The story begins with a group of eco-terrorists finding against a mega corporation with its own paramilitary that is in the resource extraction industry and ends with saving the planet from climate disaster via the power of friendship! It’s a bit on the nose, but has resonated with millions of fans around the globe. The game is a 10 out of 10 by almost any metric and it solidified Square’s place in the annals of gaming history.

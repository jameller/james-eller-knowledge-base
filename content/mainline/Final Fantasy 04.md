# Significance
This was my introduction to the franchise. I was still a bit too young to really understand the depths of the mechanics, but I watched an older friend play through a significant portion.

This game marks a technological leap forward, being the first Final Fantasy made for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System. Due to the system’s computational power and cartridge storage capacity the team was able to dedicate a large portion of the experience to storytelling both visually and textually. At its core Final Fantasy IV is a globe spanning tale of redemption for the central protagonist, Cecil. The themes are often more heavy than its 16 bit graphics may indicate, delving into complex topics such as imperialism, the state’s monopolized justification for violence, suicide, and how to mourn the senseless death of friends and family at the hands of black flag ‘terrorist’ attacks. It’s not all doom and gloom however. There are plenty of moments of levity throughout the story and many of the franchise’s cuter aesthetic elements were solidified here.

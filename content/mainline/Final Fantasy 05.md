# Significance
The original Super Nintendo Entertainment System version of this entry was never officially released in the US. However, I was able to play a port of it in the Final Fantasy Anthology compilation release. 

There are rumors that, at this time in the development cycle, even numbered entries were to be story focused and odd numbered entries were supposed to be systems focused. That being said, I’ve been unable to find anything official on this matter. It is undeniable however that this entry’s story is significantly more traditional than Final Fantasy IV. There are still some themes around loss and generational burden but the storytelling is less dramatic. We have only four characters here instead of the twelve that were featured in Final Fantasy IV. It’s a slow burn, but as I grow older I find that its themes resonate more than they did in my youth.

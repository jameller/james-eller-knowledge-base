# Significance
I have only ever played the 3D remake of this game. It’s a bit of a mystery to most US players because, unlike Final Fantasy II, it didn’t receive any direct ports until the Pixel Remaster version. It extends the job system from Final Fantasy by adding the ability to swap jobs. This is a personal favorite system of mine when it’s included in mainline entries so I certainly appreciate this game for making that a series staple!
# New or Unique Features
- Job swapping
- Introduced Moogles
- Status affects required during traversal

# Significance
Final Fantasy IX has a mixed reputation. For some, it’s considered the purest expression of the series up to the point it was released. For others, it’s considered aesthetically interesting but relatively bland in terms of storytelling and individual character arcs. Instead of attempting to create realistic character models as was done in [[Final Fantasy 08|FF8]], the team went with an extremely stylized approach in which characters have uniquely proportioned features and the color palette produces an almost cozy affect in players. It evokes nostalgia without referencing anything concrete beyond the series staples like moogles and chocobos. This is its greatest strength, in my assessment, but can leave some players feeling as if it’s more concerned with style and vibes whereas [[Final Fantasy 08|FF8]] and [[Final Fantasy 07|FF7]] were more focused on interpersonal dynamics and hard-hitting political commentary. It’s a strong entry in its own right, but stands out among the entries surrounding it because it is so much more ‘old-school’ whereas the series had been building up a reputation for trend-setting and boundary pushing over the last three entries.

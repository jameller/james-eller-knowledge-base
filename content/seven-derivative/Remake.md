# Personal Thoughts
After 23 years Square Enix released a remake that had been talked about among fans for well over a decade. There is almost no way that this game could please every fan. The directors leaned into this fact and created a truly unique experience. The key plot points from the first section of the original [[Final Fantasy 07|Final Fantasy VII]] are here as well as the major locations. However, there's so much new content and alternative plot threads that it truly is a fundamentally different experience. Playing this game is not a substitute for the original. And that's a good thing. It's a unique experience that contains the core that made the original so fantastic while blazing its own path forward. This remake will have two direct sequels of its own. I can't wait to see where they go, even though I don't feel comfortable claiming this to be an authentic remake of the original icon.

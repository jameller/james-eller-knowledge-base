# Personal Thoughts
This is a decidedly less mature game than [[Final Fantasy Tactics]] on the PlayStation! That being said, the game mechanics are still very complicated. They're just contextualized and delivered in a way that may be easier to grasp for younger audiences. I don't believe I ever completed it, but I do remember that it certainly made bus rides to and from school much more enjoyable.

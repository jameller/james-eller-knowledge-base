# Personal Thoughts
Square really went out on a limb with this game, and they really delivered something unique! While not exactly grimdark, the aesthetics and story really do lean into adult fantasy and even a bit of body horror. It's mature and through and through, lacking many of the goofy breaks that the mainline games include to keep things from becoming too dramatic.

The central mechanics and tactical gameplay style also lend themselves to creating a more mature vibe. There is a steep learning curve to this game and it does not ease players in gently. The entire game is about planning for tactical engagements and precisely executing on that plan. It was hard as nails when I was young, but the story was so strong that I struggled through!
